# Pre-Proposal Template 

*Proposed Modifications to Spring Run Juvenile Production Estimate (SR JPE) Models*

This template is for a **pre-proposal** for modifications to SR JPE models. Less detail is expected in a pre-proposal than a full proposal. Please provide one form per modification and submit to the Science Coordinator (currently, Brett Harvey, DWR; brett.harvey@water).

This template is based on the [CVPIA SIT](https://iris.fws.gov/APPS/ServCat/DownloadFile/299567) template and process. The SR JPE Model Modification Process is described here.

1. **Proposal Title.** Provide concise 1-5 word title for proposal

2. **Proposal Sponsor Names and Contact Info.** Provide name, affiliation, email, phone number for each proposal sponsor

3. **Date of Submission**

4. **Name of the SR JPE Model that is being modified**

5. **Issue of Concern.** Provide a concise narrative describing the issue of concern (why you are suggesting this modification).

6. **Proposed Change to the Model.** Include a diagram of the model change. If applicable and able, identify parameters or data values affected.

7. **Scientific Rationale for the Proposed Change to the Model.** Include specific hypotheses and support from published/unpublished references or ongoing research and monitoring activities.

8. **Citation(s) of Documents Supporting the Proposed Change to the Model.** Provide a full citation as would appear in a published journal article, and provide copies of the reference.

# Full Proposal Template 

*Proposed Modifications to Spring Run Juvenile Production Estimate (SR JPE) Models*

This template is for a **full proposal** for modifications to SR JPE models.  The full proposal should contain more detail than the pre-proposal and specific instructions for implementing the proposed change. Please provide one form per modification and submit to the Science Coordinator (currently, Brett Harvey, DWR; brett.harvey@water).

This template is based on the [CVPIA SIT](https://iris.fws.gov/APPS/ServCat/DownloadFile/299567) template and process. The SR JPE Model Modification Process is described here.

1. **Proposal Title.** Provide concise 1-5 word title for proposal

2. **Proposal Sponsor Names and Contact Info.** Provide name, affiliation, email, phone number for each proposal sponsor

3. **Date of Submission**

4. **Name of the SR JPE Model that is being modified**

5. **Issue of Concern.** Provide a concise narrative describing the issue of concern (why you are suggesting this modification).

6. **Diagram of Proposed Change to the Model.** Include a diagram of the model change. If applicable, identify parameters or data values affected.

7. **Proposed Change to the Model** Provide detailed and specific changes to the model, and identify parameter or data values when applicable. More detail should be included here than in the pre-proposal stage.

8. **Scientific Rationale for the Proposed Change to the Model.** Include specific hypotheses and support from published/unpublished references or ongoing research and monitoring activities.

9. **Instructions on How to Integrate Proposed Change into the Model** Include specific and detailed instructions for implementing the proposed change. Consult with technical staff who are familiar with the models where needed.

10. **Citation(s) of Documents Supporting the Proposed Change to the Model.** Provide a full citation as would appear in a published journal article, and provide copies of the reference.
