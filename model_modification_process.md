# SR JPE Model Modification Process

The SR JPE incorporates the best available science and data which requires models to be modified over time. The SR JPE Modification Process is based on the [CVPIA SIT two-step proposal process](https://iris.fws.gov/APPS/ServCat/DownloadFile/299567) for change.

## Pre-Proposal

The process is initiated when a Core Team member (proposal sponsor) reaches out to the Science Coordinator (currently, Brett Harvey, DWR; brett.harvery@water.ca.gov) with their idea for a model modification. The Science Coordinator will review the idea and provide information about the process, deadlines, and timing for engaging the Core Team.

The proposal sponsor will fill out a [pre-proposal](https://github.com/SRJPE/system-design-docs/blob/main/model_modification_proposal_template.md) with information regarding:

- The scientific rationale for the proposed modifications
- A diagram describing the changes
- A list of sources that can be used to inform the proposed modification

The pre-proposal will be submitted to the Science Coordinator.

The Core Team will be provided with the pre-proposal and will meet (or a smaller subgroup will meet) to discuss the proposed modification. Proposal sponsors are responsible for participating in the discussion and answering any questions.

The Science Coordinator will reach out to the Core Team to determine if a full proposal is warranted.

## Full Proposal

If asked to develop a full proposal, the proposal sponsor will complete the [full proposal template](https://github.com/SRJPE/system-design-docs/blob/main/model_modification_proposal_template.md). The full proposal should contain more detail than the pre-proposal and specific instructions for implementing the proposed change. The Science Coordinator will review the proposal for completeness and will coordinate the proposal evaluation process.

The Core Team will be provided with the proposal and will evaluate it based on the scientific rigor of the proposed modification while considering the value of additional complexity and resources required to implement the modification. The proposal will be evaluated in the context of the SR JPE program goals. If in favor of the change, the Core Team will decide on how the prototype will be evaluated.

If evaluated favorably, the Data and Modeling Team will be directed to prototype the modifications. Proposal sponsors are responsible for answering questions and supporting the prototyping.

The Core Team will evaluate the prototype following the criteria decided upon and based on this evaluation the prototype will be accepted or rejected. If accepted, the modifications will be finalized by the Data and Modeling Team and integrated into the models.
